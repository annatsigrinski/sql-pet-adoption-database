Shelter_location
    location_id – Integer, primary key. Unique ID for each shelter location
    name – Text. Name of the shelter
    address – Text. Physical address of the shelter
    capacity – Integer. Maximum number of animals the shelter can hold

Staff
    staff_id – Integer, primary key. Unique ID for each staff member
    name – Text. Full name of the staff member
    role – Text. Role or job title (Manager, Veterinarian, Staff)
    location_id – Integer, foreign key. References Shelter_location.location_id. Indicates the shelter where the staff member works

Adopter
    adopter_id – Integer, primary key. Unique ID for each adopter
    name – Text. Full name of the adopter
    phone_number – Text. Contact phone number
    email – Text. Contact email address
    address – Text. Home address of the adopter

Animal
    animal_id – Integer, primary key. Unique ID for each animal
    name – Text. Name of the animal
    species – Text. Animal species (Dog, Cat)
    breed – Text. Breed of the animal
    age – Integer. Age of the animal in years
    status – Text. Adoption status (Available, Adopted, Pending)
    intake_date – Date. Date when the animal was taken into the shelter
    location_id – Integer, foreign key. References Shelter_location.location_id. Indicates where the animal is currently located

Application
    application_id – Integer, primary key. Unique ID for each adoption application
    animal_id – Integer, foreign key. References Animal.animal_id. The animal being applied for
    adopter_id – Integer, foreign key. References Adopter.adopter_id. The person applying to adopt
    staff_id – Integer, foreign key. References Staff.staff_id. Staff member who processed the application
    application_date – Date. When the application was submitted
    status – Text. Status of the application (Approved, Pending)

Vet_check
    vet_check_id – Integer, primary key. Unique ID for each veterinary check
    animal_id – Integer, foreign key. References Animal.animal_id. The animal examined
    staff_id – Integer, foreign key. References Staff.staff_id. Veterinarian or staff member who performed the check
    check_date – Date. Date of the veterinary check
    check_type – Text. Type of check (Vaccination, Check-up, Dental check, etc.)
    notes – Text. Additional notes about the check
